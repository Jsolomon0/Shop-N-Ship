How Shop N Ship earns

Store price + transparent service.

Merchandise is the shopping base. Shop N Ship charges a service fee for sourcing and operating the platform, plus a shopper-store fee when a physical shopper is required. Freight, Customs/VAT and Nassau delivery remain visible line items.

1
Service fee

Tiered by merchandise value so expensive electronics are not overcharged.

2
Physical shopper fee

Applied to Ross, Burlington, TJ Maxx and similar store-level sourcing.

3
Shipping & delivery

Quoted separately so customers understand the true landed cost.
